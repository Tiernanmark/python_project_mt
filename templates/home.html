Welcome to the Fraiser episode log

Looking for your favourite Frasier episode? Or perhaps you're simply trying to settle the age-old question: Who is the best Crane family member? Well, you've come to the right place.

Here you can browse and search through the wonderful world of Frasier, find your favourite episodes, relive some of Seattle's finest radio advice, and vote for the character who deserves your undying admiration.

Frasier follows Dr. Frasier Crane, a sophisticated psychiatrist who returns to Seattle to host a radio advice show — because apparently talking about everyone else's problems is easier than dealing with his own.

Along the way, Frasier is joined by his eccentric family, including his grumpy father Martin, his competitive brother Niles, and a collection of friends, colleagues, and questionable romantic decisions.

From elaborate misunderstandings and sibling rivalries to expensive restaurants and even more expensive mistakes, Frasier delivers clever humour, unforgettable characters, and plenty of moments that make you laugh, cringe, and occasionally wonder how these people are allowed to make their own decisions.

So grab a cup of coffee, settle in, and I'm listening...
