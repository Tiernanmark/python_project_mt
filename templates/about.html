The History of Frasier Crane

Dr. Frasier Crane is one of television’s most recognisable characters, first appearing in Cheers in 1984, played by Kelsey Grammer. He was introduced as a sophisticated psychiatrist who became involved with the regulars at Sam Malone’s Boston bar. Although intelligent and confident in his professional life, Frasier was also insecure, competitive and often overly analytical, making him a natural source of comedy.

Frasier eventually married psychiatrist Lilith Sternin, and they had a son, Frederick. When Cheers ended in 1993, Frasier moved to Seattle and became the star of the spin-off Frasier.

The original Frasier series followed him as a radio psychiatrist while he lived with his retired police officer father, Martin, and his brother Niles, who was also a psychiatrist. The contrast between Frasier’s sophisticated personality and Martin’s practical outlook created much of the show’s humour. Frasier also struggled with relationships, loneliness and his constant desire for success and perfection.

The series ran for eleven seasons before ending in 2004, with Frasier leaving Seattle to pursue a new life.

Almost twenty years later, Frasier returned in the 2023 revival series. Now older, he has moved back to Boston, where his story originally began. He is attempting to build a relationship with his adult son Freddy while adjusting to a new stage of life.

From Cheers to the modern series, Frasier has evolved from a young, ambitious psychiatrist into a more mature and reflective father, while retaining the wit, sophistication and neuroses that made him such a beloved character.
